About The App

Car Troubleshooting Assistant is a powerful mobile application designed to help mechanics and car owners instantly decode, understand, and fix automotive trouble codes efficiently. Built with modern cross-platform technology, it offers a seamless experience across all your devices.

Trouble Code Search

Instantly search and decode thousands of OBD2 codes to identify exact vehicle issues with detailed, easy-to-understand explanations.

Interactive Schematics

Access high-quality wiring diagrams and schematics directly on your device to trace electrical issues accurately.

Free Access

Enjoy our extensive database for free. The app is supported by minimally intrusive advertisements for your convenience.

Secure Authentication

Keep your diagnostic history and preferences safely backed up using secure, industry-standard sign-in mechanisms.
